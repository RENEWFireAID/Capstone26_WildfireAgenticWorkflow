How many images support each summer?

Coverage tells us where data exist. Observation counts show how much imagery supports each location.

SummerApprox. median inputsNo inputs1–2 inputsValid NDVI

Counts require QA-screened red, NIR and SWIR2 bands in the same image. They are image inputs, not distinct days or independent measurements. The approximate median count uses all analysis pixels, including zeros; area percentages use the whole fire boundary.

The 1–2 column is a descriptive flag, not a validated minimum-data rule. NDVI is calculated from median reflectance and may add its own masks, so usable input counts and valid-index coverage answer different questions.

Sources, methods and limits
  • NASA HLS L30 and S30: QA-masked median reflectance, June 15 inclusive to August 15 exclusive. Pre-fire baseline 2018; post-fire NDVI 2020–2024.
  • Alaska Fire History: selected fire boundaries from the shared project asset. Swan Lake’s perimeter was incorporated from MTBS; it is not an independent boundary reference.
  • Dynamic World: mean pre-fire tree-class probability, not percent tree cover.
  • USGS 3DEP with Copernicus GLO-30 fallback: elevation, slope and northness. Copernicus DEM © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.
  • TerraClimate: summer temperature/precipitation anomalies, 2020–2024 versus 1991–2020. Coarse climate cells are not independent 30 m observations.
  • USFS/USGS MTBS: separate severity reference comparisons, not ground truth. This viewer plots HLS-derived sample values; it does not display MTBS raster classes.
  • OLS predicts each sample’s linear NDVI slope. Environmental predictors: dNBR, elevation, slope, northness, tree-class probability, temperature anomaly and precipitation anomaly. All samples have equal weight; these are not area-weighted fire estimates.
  • Initial NDVI is from 2020 and shares a year with the 2020-start response. Climate covers the response period. These retrospective comparisons are not prospective forecasts or evidence of causation.
  • Residual spatial dependence remains in all full-model fire/window fits. Ordinary OLS significance and Alaska-wide accuracy are not established. Three selected fires from one year cannot demonstrate statewide generalization.
